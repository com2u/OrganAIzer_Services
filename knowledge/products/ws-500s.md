# Auerswald COMfortel WS-500S — Teleprofi product knowledge

> The **DECT base (infrastructure)** behind the cordless handsets. This entry teaches
> **how Teleprofi designs a cordless system** — coverage, roaming, capacity,
> single-cell vs. multi-cell — **not** which handset to fit (that's
> [`comfortel-m730.md`](./comfortel-m730.md) / [`comfortel-m710.md`](./comfortel-m710.md),
> referenced, not duplicated). Reusable principles live in `business-philosophy/`.
>
> **Knowledge maturity: v0.1 (draft).** Teleprofi-specific gaps are precise questions
> under **[Teleprofi Knowledge Needed](#teleprofi-knowledge-needed)**.

# Executive Summary

**What is it?** Auerswald's **single-cell IP-DECT base station** — **one base, one
coverage cell**, supporting **up to 20 handsets** and **up to 8 simultaneous calls**
(Auerswald). It connects the COMfortel DECT handsets (M-730 / M-710) to the COMtrexx
PBX. It can later be **licence-upgraded into a multi-cell system**, but on its own it
is single-cell (Auerswald).

**Who is it for?** Sites needing cordless telephony in **one coverage area** — a
single floor/office/zone — within the **20-handset / 8-call** envelope.

**Why does Teleprofi sell it?** It's the **current single-cell DECT base**
(`teleprofi_fulda.md`) and the right-sized infrastructure where one cell covers the
site. DECT planning/coverage is a core Teleprofi service (`teleprofi_fulda.md`, DECT
Infrastructure).

# Teleprofi Recommendation

Recommend the **WS-500S where one base covers the required area** and the site stays
within **≤20 handsets / ≤8 simultaneous calls**, with **no need for seamless roaming
across multiple bases**. The choice is **infrastructure-driven** — decided by a
**coverage survey** (`services/dect-site-survey.md`), not by the handset. Where the
area is large/multi-zone or capacity is higher, a **multi-cell** design (WS-500M) is
the answer (see "When another solution…").

# Typical Customer

Small-to-mid sites that need cordless mobility in **one zone** — offices, practices,
small retail/workshops — already on (or getting) a COMtrexx PBX (`teleprofi_fulda.md`).
The handset (rugged M-730 vs office M-710) is chosen separately by environment. (How
often a single WS-500S suffices vs. needing multi-cell — Knowledge Needed Q1.)

# Typical Deployment

One WS-500S placed for best coverage of the target area, connected to the COMtrexx
PBX, with COMfortel handsets registered to it. Teleprofi's DECT work routinely
includes **coverage planning, coverage testing, base placement, device registration
and user onboarding** (`teleprofi_fulda.md`, DECT Infrastructure;
`services/dect-site-survey.md`) — the WS-500S is the base sized in that process.

# DECT Infrastructure Philosophy

How Teleprofi **designs a cordless system** (the lesson this entry teaches):

1. **Survey first.** Coverage is a building-physics problem — walls, floors, distance,
   interference. Teleprofi plans and **tests coverage** before committing
   (`teleprofi_fulda.md`; `services/dect-site-survey.md`), per [[installation-philosophy]].
2. **Size to area + handsets + calls.** Pick the base topology from the coverage area,
   the **handset count**, and the **simultaneous-call** need.
3. **Single-cell vs. multi-cell** is the core decision:
   - **One zone, ≤20 handsets, ≤8 calls, no inter-base roaming → WS-500S** (single-cell).
   - **Large/multi-zone, seamless roaming across bases, more handsets/calls → multi-cell
     (WS-500M)** — up to 250 handsets / 60 calls (Auerswald).
4. **Plan the growth path.** A WS-500S can be **licence-upgraded into a multi-cell
     system** later (Auerswald) — useful when a site may expand ([[growth-planning-philosophy]]).

The **handset** choice (M-730 vs M-710) is a **separate, environment-driven** decision
layered on top of this infrastructure (see the handset entries).

# Strengths

- **Single-cell simplicity:** one base, easy to deploy where one cell covers the site.
- **Up to 20 handsets / 8 simultaneous calls** (Auerswald) — ample for many small sites.
- **Native Auerswald DECT** — integrates with COMtrexx + COMfortel handsets as one
  ecosystem (management/support coherence).
- **Upgrade path:** licence-upgradeable toward multi-cell (Auerswald).

# Limitations

- **Single-cell:** **not suitable for building/expanding a multi-cell system** on its
  own — large-area roaming needs the WS-500M (Auerswald).
- **No seamless inter-base roaming** (one base = one cell).
- Capacity ceiling **20 handsets / 8 simultaneous calls** — beyond that, multi-cell.
- Coverage depends on **placement** (a survey concern, not a base flaw).

# Coverage considerations

One WS-500S covers **one cell**; effective range depends on building materials and
layout. A **survey** determines whether a single base reaches the whole area or whether
multiple bases (multi-cell, WS-500M) are required. Don't promise coverage without
measuring (`services/dect-site-survey.md`).

# Roaming considerations

The WS-500S is **single-cell → no handover between bases**. If handsets must move
**seamlessly across a large area served by several bases**, that is a **multi-cell**
(WS-500M) requirement — the WS-500S can be **licence-upgraded into** such a system, or
the design starts on WS-500M. (Teleprofi's real single-vs-multi threshold — Knowledge
Needed Q2.)

# Capacity considerations

**Up to 20 handsets and 8 simultaneous calls** per WS-500S (Auerswald). Size to
**peak concurrent calls**, not just handset count — a site with 15 handsets but heavy
simultaneous use may approach the 8-call limit. Beyond 20 handsets or 8 calls →
multi-cell (WS-500M: up to 250 / 60).

# When Teleprofi recommends the WS-500S

One coverage zone, **≤20 handsets**, **≤8 simultaneous calls**, **no multi-base
roaming** needed — the right-sized single-cell base.

# When another solution is more appropriate

- **Large/multi-zone coverage or seamless roaming** → **multi-cell WS-500M** (or a
  WS-500S licence-upgraded into multi-cell) (Auerswald).
- **>20 handsets or >8 simultaneous calls** → multi-cell.
- **Gigaset N670** is an **alternative DECT base** Teleprofi also supports
  (`teleprofi_fulda.md`); when Teleprofi chooses Auerswald WS-500 vs. Gigaset N670 is
  an open question (Knowledge Needed Q3) — likely ecosystem-coherence with
  Auerswald/COMtrexx, to confirm.

# Common customer questions

- "How many cordless phones can it handle?" → up to 20 handsets / 8 at once (Auerswald).
- "Will one base cover our whole building?" → depends — needs a coverage survey.
- "Can people walk around the whole site on a call?" → within one cell yes; across a
  large multi-base area needs multi-cell (WS-500M).
- "Can we grow later?" → yes, it can be licence-upgraded into a multi-cell system.

# AI Recommendation Signals

**Lean WS-500S** on a **single-zone, modest-capacity** cordless signal (one floor/area,
≤20 handsets, ≤8 calls, no roaming across bases). **Lean multi-cell (WS-500M)** on
**large-area / multi-zone / seamless-roaming / higher-capacity** signals. Decisive
signals = **coverage area + roaming need + handset/call capacity** — always confirmed
by a **survey**. The handset model is a **separate** decision (environment-driven).

# AI Conversation Example

Customer: *"We want cordless phones for our single-floor office — about ten people, and
they move between the front desk and the back rooms."*
→ A **single-zone, modest-capacity** signal → the **WS-500S** (single-cell, up to 20
handsets / 8 calls) is the right DECT base, **subject to a coverage survey** to confirm
one base reaches the back rooms (`services/dect-site-survey.md`). Handset choice is
separate — office use suggests the **M-710**, or the rugged **M-730** if any area is
harsh. *(If it were a large warehouse needing roaming across several bases, the answer
would be a multi-cell WS-500M instead.)*

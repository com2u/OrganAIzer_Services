# Teleprofi Growth Planning Philosophy

> Reusable principle. Products reference this instead of restating it. Closely
> tied to [[product-selection-philosophy]].

## Principle

Size systems to **future business needs**, not simply today's employee count. The
right product is the one that still fits as the business grows over the investment's
lifetime.

## Signals that growth planning must be taken seriously

Begin evaluating a larger / more scalable platform when one or more applies:

- Active-user count approaching the smaller system's practical comfort zone.
- Expected company growth (more employees).
- More departments.
- More internal call groups.
- More advanced call routing.
- Additional software integration.
- Multiple product lines.
- More analog devices.
- More advanced door communication / future expansion.
- Increased general expansion requirements.

## In practice

During consultation, always capture and discuss:

- Expected growth.
- Future locations / additional sites.
- Additional employees.
- Future telephony requirements and complexity.

The recommendation is then **based on future business needs**, not just the current
headcount.

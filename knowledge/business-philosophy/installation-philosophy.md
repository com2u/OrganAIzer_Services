# Teleprofi Installation Philosophy

> Reusable principle. Products reference this for their installation approach
> instead of restating it. See also [[firmware-policy]].

## Principle

Teleprofi prefers delivering systems that are **stable, reliable, easy to maintain
and ready to run**, with minimal customer downtime — over chasing the newest or
cheapest option (`teleprofi_fulda.md`, Installation Philosophy).

Preferences:

- Reliable solutions.
- Easy maintenance.
- Preconfigured equipment.
- Plug-and-play deployment.
- Long-term stability.
- Minimal customer downtime.

## Standard delivery workflow

1. **Preconfiguration** — configure the system at Teleprofi before the visit.
2. **Workshop testing** — bench-test the configuration before going on site.
3. **Customer appointment** — scheduled to minimise downtime.
4. **Installation** — site/connect the equipment.
5. **Configuration** — finalise on-site specifics.
6. **Testing** — verify the system end to end.
7. **Customer training** — leave the customer able to operate it.
8. **Documentation** — record what was installed and configured.
9. **Support** — ongoing under maintenance / remote support.

(Grounded in `teleprofi_fulda.md` Customer Engagement Process + Installation
Philosophy; product-specific checklists live in the product entry.)

## Why

Stable, preconfigured, well-documented installations reduce callbacks, keep
customers running, and make long-term support sustainable for a small technician
team — the core of Teleprofi's value.

# Teleprofi Financing Philosophy — Purchase, Rental & Leasing

> Reusable principle. Products, solutions and `ai-rules/` reference this instead of
> restating it. Captured from Teleprofi operational knowledge. **No prices here** —
> figures and terms live in `pricing/` (uses the canonical billing vocabulary).
> See also [[product-selection-philosophy]] and [[growth-planning-philosophy]].

## General principle

Teleprofi does **not** recommend a financing model based solely on the lowest
monthly payment. A financing recommendation weighs:

- the customer's financial situation,
- company size,
- expected business growth,
- expected lifetime of the system,
- future expansion,
- technology lifecycle,
- long-term total cost of ownership.

The goal is the **best long-term value for the customer**, not the cheapest-looking
monthly figure.

## Purchase

Generally recommended when:

- the customer prefers to own the equipment,
- the investment comfortably fits the available budget,
- the customer expects to use the system for many years,
- financing offers no significant business advantage.

Common for smaller installations and customers who prefer a one-time investment.

## Rental

Suitable when:

- the customer prefers predictable monthly costs,
- flexibility matters more than ownership,
- capital should stay available for the core business,
- the customer wants to reduce the initial investment.

**Never recommend rental only because the monthly payment looks lower.**

## Leasing / financing (Grenke)

Teleprofi offers leasing through **Grenke**. Grenke finances the equipment while
**Teleprofi remains responsible for consulting, installation, configuration,
migration and support**. This lets customers obtain professional systems with
manageable monthly payments, while Teleprofi is paid through the financing process.

## Can financing influence the technical recommendation?

**Yes — but only to enable a better solution, never to replace technical judgement.**

Sometimes a customer initially asks for a smaller system because of budget limits.
If leasing makes a **significantly better long-term solution** attainable, Teleprofi
may recommend the larger system.

> Example: instead of recommending COMtrexx Next purely because of today's budget,
> **leasing may make COMtrexx Flex affordable** and avoid replacing the PBX in a few
> years (see `products/comtrexx-family.md`).

**Technical suitability always comes first.** Financing *supports* the
recommendation; it does not drive the architecture.

## When to avoid certain financing options

Depends on the customer's situation — e.g.:

- very small installations where financing provides little benefit,
- customers who explicitly prefer ownership,
- customers with existing investment policies,
- cases where purchase is financially more advantageous.

**Never push a financing model.** Explain the available options with their
advantages and disadvantages, and let the customer choose.

## Decision order (the principle)

1. **Determine the technically correct solution.**
2. **Determine the commercially appropriate solution.**
3. **Select the financing model** that best fits the customer's business.

> The financing model must **never determine the technical architecture.**

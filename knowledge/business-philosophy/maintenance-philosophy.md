# Teleprofi Maintenance Philosophy

> Reusable principle. Products and services reference this instead of restating it.
> See also [[firmware-policy]] and [[installation-philosophy]].

## Principle

Keeping a customer's systems **stable and current is a professional service**, not
an afterthought. Maintenance is part of the long-term customer relationship and is
**billed as a service where applicable** (Teleprofi operational knowledge).

This covers, among other things:

- Firmware updates, performed under the [[firmware-policy]] (only proven-stable
  firmware; evaluate new releases first).
- Ongoing support, troubleshooting and care of installed systems.

## In practice

- Offer maintenance/support as an ongoing service alongside the installation, not
  only on a break/fix basis.
- Delivery vehicles: `services/maintenance-contract.md` (ongoing contract) and
  `services/remote-support.md` (ad-hoc remote work).
- Pricing/billing terms live in `pricing/` — never embedded in knowledge entries.

## Why

A small technician team supporting many sites stays sustainable when systems are
kept stable and maintained proactively. Proactive maintenance reduces emergencies,
protects uptime, and deepens the long-term relationship — consistent with
Teleprofi's reliability-first approach (`teleprofi_fulda.md`).

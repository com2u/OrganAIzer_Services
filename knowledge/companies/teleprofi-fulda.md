# Teleprofi Fulda — Company Profile

> Canonical **business knowledge** for Teleprofi Fulda, for AI agents that need to
> understand what the company does, what it sells, the problems it solves, and how
> it communicates. This is durable business knowledge — not technical configuration.
> Technical/runtime details (addresses, extensions, orbits) live in their own
> sources and are intentionally **not** duplicated here.

## Overview

Teleprofi Fulda is a telecommunications, networking, and communication
infrastructure company serving primarily business customers. The focus is on
practical, reliable, supportable solutions.

## Target Customers

Primary target:

- Small businesses
- Professional offices
- Retail businesses
- Service companies
- Medical practices
- Organizations requiring telephony and network infrastructure

Typical size:

- 5–40 employees

Also supported:

- 1–5 employee businesses
- Small office / home office environments

## Licensing Rules

Important business rule: certain telephony systems are licensed in blocks.

Examples:

- Up to 5 users requires a 5-user package
- More than 5 users may require a 10-user package

The customer should always be asked:

- Current number of users
- Planned growth
- Future expansion requirements

> **Needs Human Confirmation:** exact licensing block sizes and thresholds depend
> on the specific system and vendor terms; confirm per product before quoting.

## Installation Philosophy

Teleprofi prefers:

- Reliable solutions
- Easy maintenance
- Preconfigured equipment
- Plug-and-play deployment
- Long-term stability
- Minimal customer downtime

## How recommendations should be made

Recommendations should follow the company's priorities above: favor reliable,
supportable, easy-to-maintain solutions from the supported manufacturers; size
licensing to current users plus planned growth; and offer purchase, rental, or
leasing as appropriate. Keep advice honest and non-salesy, resolve immediately when
possible, and escalate when required.

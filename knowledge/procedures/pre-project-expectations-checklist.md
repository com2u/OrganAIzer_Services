# Pre-Project Expectations Checklist

> What must be clarified with the customer before a project begins. This is
> the step-by-step "what to confirm before kickoff" companion to
> [`../business-philosophy/installation-philosophy.md`](../business-philosophy/installation-philosophy.md),
> which owns the delivery workflow itself (preconfig → bench test →
> install → ... → support). This checklist sits immediately upstream of
> that workflow.

## When to use

After an offer is accepted, before installation work (or its preparation
phase) begins.

## Preconditions

The offer has been accepted (see `sales-offer-checklist.md`).

## Steps

> Candidate list — from the interview draft's "Welche Erwartungen setzen wir
> immer vor Projektbeginn?" answer, not yet confirmed.

Before a project starts, the following must be clear to both sides:

1. Leistungsumfang (scope of work).
2. Verantwortlichkeiten (responsibilities — who does what).
3. Technische Voraussetzungen (technical prerequisites).
4. Mitwirkung des Kunden (what the customer must contribute).
5. Benötigte Zugänge (access needed — network, premises, systems).
6. Ausfall- oder Umschaltfenster (downtime/cutover windows).
7. Terminabhängigkeiten (scheduling dependencies).
8. Laufende Lizenzen und Kosten (recurring licences/costs).
9. Funktionen, die im Angebot enthalten oder nicht enthalten sind (which
   functions the offer does/doesn't include).
10. Abnahmeprozess (acceptance process).
11. Dokumentation und Einweisung (documentation and training/handover).
12. Support nach der Installation (post-installation support).

## Validation

All 12 items above are explicitly confirmed with the customer (not merely
assumed) before installation begins.

## Rollback / if it fails

If any item is unclear or unconfirmed, resolve it with the customer before
starting work rather than discovering the gap mid-installation.

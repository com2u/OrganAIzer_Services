# Sales Offer (Angebot) Checklist

> What a Teleprofi commercial offer must contain, what must never happen in
> one, and when to deliberately withhold or delay one. This is the
> step-by-step "how" companion to
> [`../business-philosophy/sales-qualification-philosophy.md`](../business-philosophy/sales-qualification-philosophy.md),
> which holds the reasoning; this file holds the checklist. Keep them
> distinct — do not duplicate content between the two.

## When to use

Before any commercial offer (Angebot) is sent to a customer.

## Preconditions

The needs-analysis and (where required) site-visit stages are complete — see
`sales-qualification-philosophy.md` and `site-visit-checklist.md`.

## Steps — mandatory offer contents

> From the interview draft's "ANGEBOTE" answers.

Every offer should contain:

1. Klare Leistungsbeschreibung (a clear description of what is being
   delivered).
2. Produkte und Mengen (products and quantities).
3. Lizenzen (licences).
4. Dienstleistungen (services).
5. Voraussetzungen (prerequisites).
6. Mitwirkungspflichten (what the customer must provide/do).
7. Laufende Kosten (recurring costs).
8. Ausschlüsse (explicit exclusions).
9. Optionen (options).
10. Liefer- und Terminannahmen (delivery/scheduling assumptions).
11. Abnahme (acceptance process).
12. Dokumentation (documentation).
13. Support oder Wartung (support/maintenance).
14. Gültigkeit des Angebots (offer validity period).

## Steps — mistakes that must never happen in an offer

> Same source.

- Falsche Mengen (wrong quantities).
- Fehlende Pflichtlizenzen (missing mandatory licences).
- Nicht erklärte laufende Kosten (unexplained recurring costs).
- Versprochene, ungeprüfte Funktionen (promising features that weren't
  verified).
- Nicht kalkulierte Arbeitsleistungen (uncosted labour).
- Unklare Verantwortlichkeiten (unclear responsibilities).
- Falsche Rufnummern oder Standorte (wrong numbers or locations).
- Fehlende Voraussetzungen (missing prerequisites).
- Widersprüchliche Positionen (contradictory line items).
- Nicht sichtbare Ausschlüsse (exclusions that aren't clearly visible).

## Validation

An offer is ready to send only when (from the interview draft's "would you
send this offer as-is?" framing — see also
`knowledge/procedures/ai-recommendation-review-checklist.md` if/when that is
created for AI-assisted offers specifically):

- All assumptions are visible.
- Open points are marked as open, not silently assumed resolved.
- No feature is presented as guaranteed if it still needs checking.
- The customer can tell what they are getting and what they are not.
- A Teleprofi staff member takes responsibility for releasing it.

## Rollback / if it fails

If any mandatory item is missing or any of the never-do mistakes is present,
the offer is not sent. Return to the missing information stage rather than
sending a partial or assumption-laden offer.

## When to deliberately withhold or delay an offer

> Same source.

- Wesentliche technische Informationen fehlen (key technical information is
  missing).
- Der Bedarf ist widersprüchlich (the need as stated is contradictory).
- Ein Vor-Ort-Termin ist erforderlich, aber noch nicht erfolgt (a required
  site visit hasn't happened yet).
- Lieferbarkeit oder Produktfreigabe ist unklar (delivery/product approval
  is unclear).
- Eine Schnittstelle wurde nicht geprüft (an integration/interface hasn't
  been checked).
- Der Kunde hat noch keine verantwortliche Entscheidung getroffen (no
  responsible decision-maker has committed yet).
- Der Aufwand ist nicht seriös kalkulierbar (effort can't be honestly
  estimated yet).
- Das gewünschte Ergebnis erscheint technisch oder wirtschaftlich nicht
  sinnvoll (the desired outcome doesn't make technical or commercial sense).

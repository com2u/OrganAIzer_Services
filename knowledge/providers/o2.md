# Overview

> o2 / Telefónica — a German carrier offering business All-IP telephony via "o2
> Business Voice SIP", connecting a customer's IP PBX through Telefónica's IP
> network.

o2 supplies SIP telephony that products depend on. Described objectively here;
recommendation logic lives in
[`../ai-rules/provider-selection.md`](../ai-rules/provider-selection.md).

# Business Position

o2 (Telefónica Germany) offers business All-IP telephony under **o2 Business Voice
SIP**, aimed at companies running their own IP PBX that need multiple voice
channels. Per o2's documentation, voice traffic runs over **Telefónica's IP
network in Germany rather than the public Internet**. It is an established
alternative carrier for business SIP telephony.

# Customer View

## Benefits
- All-IP SIP telephony for a business PBX with flexible, scalable voice channels.
- Voice carried over Telefónica's German IP network (not the public Internet),
  per o2 documentation.
- Master number with a number block / custom numbering plan (o2 documentation).

## Typical customers
- Companies with their own IP PBX needing multiple voice channels.
- Customers who already have o2 business connectivity and want telephony on the
  same provider.

## Limitations
- Marketed primarily as a SIP voice product; Internet access pairing and fiber
  availability must be confirmed per site/offer. *(Needs confirmation.)*
- Encryption/transport specifics must be taken from o2's current SIP

# Technician View

> Only document what o2's official sources state. Do not invent specs or

## SIP compatibility
**o2 Business Voice SIP** is a SIP-Trunk connecting a business PBX to the
telephone network. Per o2: external calls require roughly **106 kbit/s of
bandwidth per voice channel**; channel count per trunk is generally not limited.
Voice runs over Telefónica's secure German IP network. Transport/encryption
parameters (TLS/SRTP, ports) must be taken from o2's current SIP specification at
configuration time. *(Confirm against o2 docs — not asserted here.)*

## Fiber compatibility
Fiber pairing/availability for o2 business connections is address-dependent and
must be confirmed per site. *(Needs confirmation.)*

## DSL compatibility
DSL/All-IP access pairing must be confirmed against o2's current business offering.
*(Needs confirmation.)*

## Router compatibility
Community/integration documentation shows AVM FRITZ!Box used with o2 All-IP
(sipbusiness.telefonica.de); confirm the specific model and configuration against
o2's current spec. *(Per-model check.)*

## PBX compatibility
Third-party notes describe SIP-capable PBXs configured against o2 All-IP Voice
SIP; validate against o2's current specification for the exact COMtrexx firmware.
*(Per-firmware check.)*

## Typical configuration considerations
- Budget ~106 kbit/s per concurrent voice channel for external calls.
- Confirm transport/encryption parameters from o2's current SIP spec before
  provisioning.

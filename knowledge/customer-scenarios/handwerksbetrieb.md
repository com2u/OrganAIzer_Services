# Typical problems

> From the interview draft's "Branche" answers.

- Mobile Mitarbeiter (mobile/field staff) are the norm, not the exception.
- Zentrale Erreichbarkeit trotz Außendienst (central reachability despite
  most staff being out on jobs).
- Weiterleitungen (call forwarding) used heavily.
- Robuste Geräte (ruggedised devices) preferred.
- Einfache Bedienung (simple operation).
- Klare Trennung von Büro und Baustelle (clear separation between office and
  job-site communication).
- Rückrufmanagement (callback management) as a named, recurring need.

# Typical problems

> Candidate content — from the interview draft's "Branche" answers, not yet
> confirmed.

- Vertraulichkeit (confidentiality) is treated as a defining requirement.
- Persönliche Zuordnung (calls/lines mapped to a specific person, not a pool).
- Vertretungsregeln (cover/deputy rules) when the responsible person is out.
- Mobile Erreichbarkeit (mobility) for lawyers working outside the office.
- Professionelle Außenwirkung (professional outward appearance/impression)
  on every call.
- Zuverlässige Dokumentation (reliable documentation) of calls/messages.
- Kontrollierte Weiterleitungen (controlled/deliberate call forwarding, not
  ad hoc).
